# MeaningsS

✅ Verified vs. ⭐ Featured – what they really mean
Verified Featured
What it does Shows a green ✅ "Verified" badge on the listing card Shows a golden ⭐ "Featured" badge and puts the listing at the very top of search results
How it's earned You (the admin) manually check that the landlord or property is real (phone call, physical visit, or you know them). It’s a trust signal for seekers. The landlord pays you for this premium visibility. It’s a monetisation lever.
Cost to landlord Free – you give it to build trust on the platform Paid – you charge a fee (e.g., UGX 15,000–30,000/month) to keep a listing featured
Your action Toggle on in admin panel when you're satisfied the listing is genuine Toggle on after receiving mobile money payment from the landlord
In short:

Verified = "This is a real, trustworthy listing."

Featured = "This landlord paid for extra exposure."

You can use both together on the same listing, or separately. Many marketplaces (Jumia, Airbnb) have similar badges
